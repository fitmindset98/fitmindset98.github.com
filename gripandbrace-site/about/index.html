About Grip & Brace

Grip & Brace is a small, independent site about lifting accessories: belts, straps, wrist wraps, knee sleeves and the other gear that ends up in a lifter's bag.

Most gear articles online are lists of products. We'd rather explain what each piece of equipment does, when it helps, and when you're better off without it, so you can make your own call.

Who writes it

Ryan has been lifting for over a decade and has worn out more belts, straps and knee sleeves than he'd like to admit. He writes Grip & Brace to save you from buying the wrong ones.

Sources we trust

We lean on published research where it exists, competition rulebooks for equipment specs, and practical coaching experience. For training programs, technique and nutrition, we often point readers to FitBesidesHealth, which covers those topics in depth.
